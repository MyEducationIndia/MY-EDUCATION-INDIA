# MY EDUCATION INDIA – End-User Manual

This manual provides instructions for Students, Faculty Members, and Administrative Staff navigating the platform.

---

## 1. Logging In with Your Institutional User ID

1. Navigate to the login portal at `https://your-campus.vercel.app/login`.
2. Enter your assigned **Institutional User ID**:
   * Students: `STU240001` (format: `STU` + 2-digit year + 4-digit sequence)
   * Faculty: `FAC240001` (format: `FAC` + 2-digit year + 4-digit sequence)
   * Staff: `EMP240001` (format: `EMP` + 2-digit year + 4-digit sequence)
3. Enter your confidential password.
4. Click **Sign In with User ID & Password**.
5. *Note on Password Recovery:* There is no public "Forgot Password" link on the portal. If you forget your password, present your campus ID card in-person to the Campus IT Helpdesk to receive an audited temporary password.

---

## 2. Student Portal Navigation

* **Dashboard:** View cumulative SGPA, current semester attendance percentage, upcoming assignments, and urgent notices.
* **Academic Enrolment:** View assigned courses, course syllabus, and lecture timetables.
* **Fee Payment:** Download fee challans, view pending installments, and make secure online tuition payments with instant receipt generation.
* **CBT Exams:** Enter the Computer-Based Testing screen during scheduled test windows. Ensure full-screen mode is maintained to avoid anti-cheat flag triggers.
* **LMS Learning:** Stream recorded HLS video lectures, submit homework assignments, participate in course Q&A forums, and chat with the AI Study Assistant.
* **Admit Cards & Hall Tickets:** Download QR-coded examination hall tickets 7 days prior to university end-semester examinations.

---

## 3. Faculty Portal Navigation

* **Course Management:** Upload syllabus modules, lecture notes, and SCORM interactive packages.
* **Attendance Registry:** Mark daily lecture attendance or review biometric RFID terminal sync logs.
* **Grading Desk:** Grade submitted student assignments with rubric criteria and qualitative feedback.
* **Marks Entry:** Enter internal assessment and practical exam marks with two-step confirmation.
* **Question Authoring:** Submit multiple-choice and descriptive questions to the central CBT Question Bank tagged by Bloom's Taxonomy levels.
